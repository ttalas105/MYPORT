# Studio record collection

The album covers below identify Thomas's requested listening collection. They are copyrighted album artwork owned by their respective rights holders; they are **not CC0, public domain, or original portfolio artwork**. No ownership, artist endorsement, or artwork license is claimed. The download source does not confer a redistribution license.

This directory contains only the seven referenced cover files listed below.

Wall-album titles, artists, and cover identities were verified against Apple's public iTunes catalog on 2026-09-08. The three table albums were verified against their Apple Music catalog pages and visually inspected on 2026-09-09. The site serves local copies and makes no runtime requests to Apple.

The four original records remain framed on the wall through `STUDIO_WALL_ALBUMS` (also exported as `STUDIO_ALBUMS` for the room builder). The loose listening-console sleeves use the separate `STUDIO_TABLE_ALBUMS` collection: Kendrick Lamar and Larry June / The Alchemist face out from the two lower cubbies, while JID stands on the right side of the tabletop, away from the turntable.
